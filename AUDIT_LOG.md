# Citation Audit Log

Every citation in this matrix was checked against primary-source text —
not secondary summaries, blog posts, or law-firm explainers.

## Method

Primary sources used:

| Source | Document |
|---|---|
| DPDP Act 2023 | Gazette-published Act text |
| DPDP Rules 2025 | Notified Rules, G.S.R. 846(E) |
| DPDP Commencement | G.S.R. 843(E), Gazette of India Extraordinary Part II–Section 3(i), No. 757, 13 November 2025 |
| GDPR | Official Journal text, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 |
| CCPA statute | California Civil Code §1798.100 et seq. |
| CCPA regulations | CPPA Regulations, §7001 et seq. |
| California breach statute | Cal. Civ. Code §1798.82 |

Three claim types were checked separately:

- **Type A — Section/Article/Rule numbers (176 claims).** Confirmed the
  cited provision exists, that the paraphrase neither adds nor drops an
  obligation, and that sub-clause ranges have the correct end-point.
- **Type B — Numeric claims (49).** Each figure verified in the clause that
  governs it, with its unit and attached conditions — not in isolation.
- **Type C — Dates and version references (99).** Checked against the
  notification instrument itself. Highest-stakes category for DPDP, because
  the Rules went through a draft stage before notification.

Note on the CCPA: the statute and the regulations are separate instruments
with independent section numbering. Rows citing both were checked against
each document separately. This distinction is what two of the four errors
below turned on.

## Result

**172 of 176 Type A citations correct on first pass (97.7%). Four errors
found and corrected.** All Type B and Type C claims checked were verified
correct.

| Row | Error | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| P4 | `§1798.140(e)(1)-(9)` — the enumerated list ends at item (8); subdivision (f) begins immediately after. Confirmed independently by CCPA Regulations §7002(c)(2), which cross-references "subdivisions (e)(1) – (e)(8)." | `§1798.140(e)(1)-(8)` |
| R6 | `§1798.140(j)` is the Civil Code's definition of **"Contractor,"** not "authorized agent." The term "authorized agent" is not defined in the statute at all — it is defined in the CCPA Regulations, a separate instrument. | `CCPA Regulations §7001(d); operative provisions at §7063` |
| P1 | `Rule 3(b)(ii)` was cited alone for a paraphrase that describes content from both sub-clauses: (i) is the itemised-personal-data requirement, (ii) is the purpose/goods-or-services requirement. | `Rule 3(b)(i)-(ii)` |
| GR1 | `Chapter V (Sections 18-28)` — Chapter V ("Data Protection Board of India") actually ends at Section 26. Sections 27-28 (the Board's inquiry and search-and-seizure-exclusion powers) fall under the separate Chapter VI ("Powers, Functions and Procedure to be Followed by Board"). | `Chapter V (Sections 18-26) and Chapter VI (Sections 27-28)` |

None of the four mischaracterised what a provision requires; each was a
citation boundary that was off by a sub-clause, a chapter, or — in R6's
case — the wrong instrument entirely. They are recorded here rather than
silently corrected, because the error rate of this kind of work is itself
useful information, and because R6 in particular was repeated in a second
location (the DSAR intake template's identity-verification section, which
also cited the Civil Code in error) — a reminder that one wrong citation
tends to propagate wherever the same concept is referenced.

## On the commencement notification

G.S.R. 843(E) underpins the commencement-status column and the headline
methodology claim, so it was verified against the Gazette PDF obtained
directly from meity.gov.in rather than any secondary source. Notification
G.S.R. 843(E), dated 13 November 2025, File No. AA-11038/1/2025-CL&ES.

A "14 November" date appears in some secondary coverage; this traces to the
PDF's digital-signature timestamp (`CG-DL-E-14112025`), not the notification
date printed on the document itself.

## Limitations of this audit

- Single-auditor review. No independent second reader.
- Checks whether citations point to the provision claimed and whether the
  paraphrase is faithful. It does not constitute legal validation of the
  comparative judgments in the Gap/Conflict column.
- Type B and Type C claims were checked at the specific clauses the audit's
  extraction pass surfaced; this is a systematic first-pass sweep, not an
  exhaustive manual re-derivation of every number independent of that
  extraction.
- Verified against the source versions listed above as at the date of this
  log. Subsequent amendments are not reflected.
